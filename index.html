How to Play

Chronanza is a daily timeline puzzle where you arrange 9 historical milestones from 1 Earliest to 9 Latest.

• Drag and drop any card onto another to swap their positions, or tap one card and then another to swap.

• Tap Check Timeline to lock in cards that are in their exact chronological slot.

• You have 4 attempts to complete the full timeline.
