How to Play

Chronanza is a daily timeline puzzle where you arrange 9 historical milestones into their correct chronological order.

• Drag and drop any tile onto another to swap their positions, or click one tile and then another to swap.

• Tap Check Timeline to lock in your correct placements. Correctly placed events lock into place.

• You have 4 attempts to complete the full timeline before game over!
